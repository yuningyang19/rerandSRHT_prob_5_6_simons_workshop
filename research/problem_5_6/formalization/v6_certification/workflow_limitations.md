# Workflow metadata boundary

The source-bound task packet is `certification_packet.json`; the installed
formalizer lifecycle-v2 preparation is separately retained. The real validator
rejects it because no complete current S8-report chain in that schema is bound.
Empty signature/dependency slots in this preparation are incomplete metadata,
not accepted mathematical signatures; the actual independent expected types,
full elaborated types and source/dependency graph live in their separate
reviewed manifests. No lifecycle-v2 PASS is asserted.

The enabled runtime exposes no authenticated workflow invocation-receipt tool.
Actual collaboration contexts, source hashes, subprocess command/exit records,
and independent reviews are retained without fabricating authenticated receipts
or retrospectively promoting historical PROBE records. The frozen expected
statements and mandatory technical tests remain required and executable.

Final-TeX technical source/diagram checks are recorded separately. Author
comprehension, originality, disclosure approval and publication authority have
not been inferred. This limitation does not change a mathematical claim,
permit an axiom, or excuse a missing proof/build/replay/export check.

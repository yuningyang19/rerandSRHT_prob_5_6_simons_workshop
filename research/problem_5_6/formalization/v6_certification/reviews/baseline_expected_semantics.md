# Reviewer-owned expected semantics

Frozen before inspecting any additive PaperV6 implementation. This document is a semantic acceptance target, not a generated dump of existing implementation types.

1. For n=2^m, 1≤r≤n and 0<ε<1, a universal C and a width k depending only on m,r,ε satisfy r≤k≤min(n,ceil(Cr/ε²)). Every fixed real n×r matrix V with VᵀV=I has probability at most 1/100 that ‖VᵀΩΩᵀV−I‖₂>ε. The probability is the uniform product law of two full independent sign vectors and an independent k-element subset. The supremum over V is outside probability. No probability guarantee may be an input hypothesis.
2. The frame class is nonempty under r≤n: embed Fin r into the n coordinate indices and use its coordinate vectors. The sample class is nonempty under k≤n. All finite probabilities then have positive denominators and total mass one. A frame probability belongs to the supremum set, which is bounded above by one, so the ordinary fixed-frame bound follows via le_csSup.
3. For each realized Ω and each z, ‖ΩᵀVz‖₂²−‖z‖₂²=zᵀ(VᵀΩΩᵀV−I)z. Since the latter matrix is symmetric, its operator norm is at most ε iff the absolute quadratic form is at most ε‖z‖₂² for every z. Both norm inequalities, including z=0, must be present.
4. Given paper edge matrices E_e with rows at dst and columns at src, instantiate B with M_e=E_eᵀ. The graph contraction is then exactly the paper sum. B graphOperator(s,t) is the transpose of the paper boundary matrix T: B(a,b)=T(b,a). Transposition preserves Euclidean operator norms. Distinct boundary vertices must be explicit for the C definition. No operator-norm conclusion belongs in a primitive graph definition.
5. The rank projection hypothesis is existence of an orthonormal factor V and exact equality P=VVᵀ on the distinguished edge. Loops count twice, edges are occurrences, weights are arbitrary real functions bounded in absolute value by one, and sums include all vertex labels without injectivity restrictions.
6. For a fixed selector equality partition π, R_{d,h}(π) consists of ALL partitions with blocks of size at least two, p−d blocks, binary constraint rank h, and neither injectivity-forbidden pair type. It does not assert nonzero cumulants or sufficiency of XOR constraints. The weight is literally product_C (2|C|)^(12|C|). All d,h≥0 are allowed; out-of-range classes are empty. The fixed-class contribution must bound the sum of absolute individual cumulant products over that class and injective labels, times the absolute selector coefficient; an already-summed master trace bound is not a replacement.
7. The signed trace conclusion is |E tr(((P−δI)(E−θI))^(2p))|≤K₀^p(δθ)^p. The absolute value remains outside the expectation. Its inputs include p≥2, 0<θ≤1/2, nθ≥r, r≥2(4p+1)^1000. It is not a norm moment or E|tr| claim.
